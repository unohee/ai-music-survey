About This Study

This is an academic research project investigating how well humans can distinguish AI-generated music from real human performances. The results contribute to ongoing research in music perception and AI audio generation.

Privacy & Data

  • No cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • Nickname is used only for the leaderboard. You can use any alias.
  • Email is entirely optional — only used if you wish to be contacted for research follow-up.
  • IP address is checked solely to prevent duplicate registrations. It is not linked to your responses.
  • All gameplay data (answers, scores) is collected anonymously for research purposes only.
  • Your data will never be sold, shared with third parties, or used for commercial purposes.

Planned Usage

  • Academic publication (ISMIR 2026)
  • Model validation for AI music detection
  • Understanding human perception vs. algorithmic detection

Open Source

This project is fully open source. You can review the entire codebase, including the server, at github.com/unohee/ai-music-survey.

Contact

For questions about this research, please open an issue on GitHub or contact the researcher directly.
